I am a senior in Computer Science at Brigham Young University, with minors in Mathematics and Physics, graduating April 2027.

I have been a research assistant in Prof. Amanda Hughes's HCI research group since November 2023.

My interests are human-centered AI reliability and evaluation, especially how AI models interpret crisis-related social media.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research and projects

  • Multimodal annotation of Hurricane Dorian tweets (first-author manuscript, in preparation for ISCRAM 2027). Five model versions from three families labeled 355 tweets with 433 photos; Priority agreement with historical TREC-IS labels ranged from 39.2% to 50.1%, and all five rated the three Critical-reference tweets as Low or Medium. These results measure agreement with historical labels, not correctness.
  • Text-to-SQL with retrieval and LoRA fine-tuning (Qwen3.5-9B, BM25), with Prof. Porter Jenkins, Fall 2025 – Winter 2026.
  • modelagree, an open-source tool for measuring agreement between LLM labels (under development).
